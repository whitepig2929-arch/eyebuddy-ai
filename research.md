# Research and source notes

## Worldwide burden: trachoma

**Source:** World Health Organization, *Trachoma*, fact sheet updated 16 July 2026.

https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/trachoma

The fact sheet reports that approximately 1.9 million people have blindness or visual impairment attributable to trachoma, an eye disease caused by infection with *Chlamydia trachomatis*.

Interpretation: a worldwide burden of vision loss associated with a specific disease. It must not be described as 1.9 million new infections per year or 1.9 million people currently infected. Trachoma should not be conflated with all conjunctivitis.

## Worldwide estimate: herpes simplex keratitis

**Paper:** *Incidence of Herpes Simplex Virus Keratitis and Other Ocular Disease: Global Review and Estimates*.

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9397127/

The research estimates approximately 1.7 million cases of HSV keratitis worldwide in 2016, including new and recurrent disease, with a 95% confidence interval of approximately 1.0–3.0 million. This is a modelled historical estimate with limited underlying incidence studies, not a current total for all eye infections.

## Conjunctivitis and diagnosis

National Eye Institute: https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/pink-eye

Conjunctivitis can have infectious and noninfectious causes. An eye examination and sometimes laboratory testing may be necessary. This supports describing EyeBuddy as a proposed screening aid, not an infection-confirming camera.
